A rule for every quirk

Your money has habits no app predicted. Describe yours once, and every transaction that fits gets handled.

Your card closes on the 26th, so anything after that counts toward next month.

If
account_name == "My credit card" and date.day >= 26 and reference_date.month == date.month
Then
Set the reference date to (reference_date + relativedelta(months=1)).replace(day=1)

Income landing in your investment account tags itself.

If
account_name == "My Investing Account" and is_income
Then
Set the tags to tag_names + ["Yield"]

Delete a subscription and its $5 cancellation fee appears as a new expense.

If
description == "Subscription A"
Then
Create an expense, amount Decimal("5.00")

Each manual contribution gets noted on this month’s goal transaction.

If
description.startswith("Manual contribution to goal:")
Then
Find “My Savings Goal” and set its notes to my_notes + f"\n- Manual contribution of ${amount} on {date.strftime('%Y-%m-%d')}"

Rules are a small subset of Python. They run when a transaction is created, updated or deleted, can change any field, and can add up other transactions with transactions().

Know your runway
At your 12-month average, how long could you go without income? It tells you, per currency.
Invent a currency

The dollar-cost averaging tracker keeps your entry price and profit.
